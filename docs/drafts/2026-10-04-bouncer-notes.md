# Category notes for the import gate — APPROVED by owner 2026-10-04

Spec 041 Part 1. Each note goes into `categories.bouncer_notes` and is read by the import gate on every new
product in that category. Line 1 is what does **not** belong (→ `wrong_category`); line 2, where present,
is what counts as a different model. Grounded in each category's rejection history and the 08-28 → 10-04
hides (prod, read 2026-10-04). Where a note follows a choice already made on a live page, the page is named.

## pw2d

**Mechanical Gaming Keyboards**
Exclude membrane and "mecha-membrane" keyboards (Razer Ornata, Logitech G213, SteelSeries Apex 3, Corsair K55), barebones or switchless kits, keyboard-and-mouse combos, mice, headsets, microphones, keycap sets and wrist rests.
Different layout (full-size, TKL, 75%, 65%) or generation (V3, V4, Gen 2) is a different model; switch type and colour are not.

**Productivity & Ergonomic Keyboards**
Exclude mice, trackballs and vertical mice, numpads, keyboard-and-mouse combos and desktop sets (Logitech MK550/MK850, Microsoft Sculpt Desktop, MX Keys Combo), DIY or switchless split kits (Corne, Sofle), and gaming-first boards sold on rapid trigger or Hall-effect switches — those belong in Mechanical Gaming Keyboards.
Different layout or generation is a different model; switch type, colour and "for Business" editions are not.

**Gaming & Chat Headsets**
Exclude webcams, earbuds and in-ear monitors, speakers, standalone microphones, headset stands and replacement ear pads. Office and call-centre headsets (Jabra Evolve2, Yealink, Poly) DO belong — the remote-worker preset is built on them.
Wired and wireless versions of the same line are different models (Cloud III ≠ Cloud III Wireless); colour and platform edition (Xbox / PlayStation) are not.

**Podcast & Studio Mics**
Exclude wireless handheld vocal systems (they belong in Lavalier & Wireless Systems), karaoke mics and sets, multi-mic packs of three or more, lavalier and shotgun mics, mic arms, pop filters, shock mounts and audio interfaces sold alone. Wired handheld dynamics (Shure SM58) DO belong — one is a live pick.
A bundle with an arm or cable is the same model as the mic alone.

**Lavalier & Wireless Systems**
Exclude shotgun and on-camera mics, wired handheld vocal mics, karaoke sets, wireless handheld vocal systems (a handheld transmitter, e.g. Shure BLX24/SM58), standalone receivers, and windscreens or clips sold alone.
Bodypack systems with a lavalier or headworn mic DO belong (the premium pick, Shure GLXD14+, is one). Transmitter/receiver count (1 TX + 1 RX, 2 TX + 1 RX) is the same model.

## coffee2decide

**Semi-Automatic & Manual Espresso Machines**
Exclude super-automatic bean-to-cup machines (no portafilter: Jura, Philips LatteGo, De'Longhi Magnifica/Dinamica/Rivelia), pod and capsule machines (Nespresso), moka pots, and drip or steam-only "espresso makers".
Portafilter machines with automated grinding or tamping (Breville Oracle Jet, Ninja Luxe Café) DO belong here.

**Super-Automatic Espresso Machines**
Exclude every portafilter machine — including ones that grind and tamp automatically (Breville Oracle, Ninja Luxe Café) — pod and capsule machines, and drip coffee makers.
Model numbers within a series that differ only by colour or finish (Jura E4 Piano White / Black) are the same model.

**Manual Coffee Grinders**
Exclude electric grinders, grain and flour mills, spice and pepper grinders, blade grinders, and replacement burrs or handles.
Burr upgrade editions (Pro, Max, ESP) are different models; colour is not.

**Gooseneck Kettles**
Exclude straight-spout kettles, teapots and ceramic kettles without a gooseneck, camping and backpacking kettles, and kettles sold only as part of a pour-over set where the dripper is the main item.
Stovetop and electric versions of the same line are different models.

**Pour-Over Drippers & Brewers**
Exclude kettles, paper filters and scales sold alone, automatic drip machines, cold brew drip towers (they belong in Cold Brew Makers), and French presses.
Size (01 / 02 / 03) and material (plastic, glass, ceramic, metal) are the same model.

**Cold Brew Makers**
Exclude hot drip machines with an "iced" or "over ice" setting (Hamilton Beach 2-Way, Black+Decker Split Brew), single-serve pod brewers (Keurig), iced-tea makers, French presses, and filters or jars sold alone.
Capacity variants of the same pitcher are the same model.
