## The one replaced pick

**Premium:** Beyerdynamic MMX 300 (Amazon now flags it "high price", so it cannot be bought at a sane price) →
**Beyerdynamic MMX 300 PRO, $380**. New text:

**Best mic and build here, still wired**

Nothing else here has a microphone like this: an integrated condenser scoring 95, broadcast-level, and the best on the list by three points over the Corsair. Build is 94, the highest score anywhere on this page, handmade in Germany with metal yokes. Noise isolation is 88 from the closed-back design alone. It's the premium pick because it tops the list on mic and build and ties the SteelSeries for isolation.

What $340 over the budget pick does not buy: wireless. It's a wired headset, and it leans on external software for virtual surround. Spatial audio is 85 on the strength of the Stellar.45 drivers' stereo imaging. The velour pads are comfortable (82), but the clamp is slightly tight out of the box. The Nova Pro Omni costs exactly the same $380 and gives you batteries, a base station and ANC instead. If your voice matters most and a cable doesn't bother you, this is where the money goes.
