## Intro

We scored all 164 lavalier and wireless mic systems in our database on six things — clarity and warmth, connection stability, setup simplicity, noise isolation, battery endurance, and build and portability — and ranked them on those scores, with price and store ratings counted in. The seven below run from $70 to $690.

Two things are worth knowing first. The DJI Mic 2 has the highest average score of the seven, and on raw scores it would win all three of our "best for" slots too. Each kit appears once here, so the Mic 2 takes the vlogger slot and the next-best kits take the other two. And yes, the budget pick costs $15 more than the best overall pick. Both sit in the cheapest price band, and the DJI Mic came first overall, so the budget slot went to the best kit left in that band.

## Best overall — DJI Mic ($85)

**Beats the Rode on noise, for $15 less**

This is the plain DJI Mic, not the Mic 2 or Mic 3 further down. It scores 85 on clarity and 90 on connection, level with the Rode Wireless Micro, and wins on noise isolation, 78 to 70, and on battery, 90 to 88. It's also $15 cheaper.

Per the store listing, the $85 kit has one transmitter and one receiver. That's fine for one person talking to a camera. For two people, look at the Rode below: its $100 kit comes with two mics and a charging case.

Build and portability is the weak point. 88 is the lowest on this page, and the Rode scores 92 there and 95 on setup. Against the $200 Mic 2 you give up 7 points of clarity and 7 of noise isolation. For most solo shoots, we'd start here.

## Best budget — Rode Wireless Micro ($100)

**Two mics and a case for $100**

Our budget pick costs $15 more than our best overall pick. Both sit in the cheapest price band here, and the DJI Mic took first overall, so this slot went to the next-best kit in that band. On the scores the two are close to a tie.

What the extra $15 gets you, per the listing: two mics and a charging case, with a USB-C plug for iPhone and Android. Per our data it's built for phone creators, and the scores agree. 95 on setup simplicity and 92 on build and portability both tie for the best on this page.

The drawback is noise. Per our data it's no noise-cancelling marvel, and 70 on noise isolation ties for the lowest here. Record beside traffic and the traffic's in the take. If you shoot in loud places, the $160 Rode Wireless GO III scores 85 on isolation.

## Premium — Shure GLXD14+/93 ($690)

**The steadiest wireless link here, at $690**

A 92 on connection stability is the best on this page. Per our data it's a dual-band system, and the connection score is where that shows. Battery endurance is 90, on a 12-hour battery. It's the best-scoring kit in the top price band.

The other scores don't follow the price. At $690 it's $430 more than the DJI Mic 3, which beats it on clarity, 88 to 85, on noise isolation, 85 to 70, and on battery, 95 to 90. Per our data the included WL93 lavalier is omnidirectional, so it picks up more of the room. Setup simplicity is 88, the lowest here.

Its 5-star rating comes from 5 reviews, so give that little weight. Buy it for presentations, where a steady link matters more than anything else. For a phone in a park, it's the wrong tool.

## Best for vloggers — DJI Mic 2 ($200)

**Highest average score of the seven**

The vlogger slot weights connection stability and setup simplicity most, then build and portability. The Mic 2 scores 88, 95 and 90 on those. It's also strong where the slot asks less: 92 on clarity, the best here, and 85 on noise isolation. Its average across all six features is the highest of the seven.

Per our data it records 32-bit float. Per the listing, the $200 kit has two transmitters, one receiver and a charging case. It's $115 more than the DJI Mic, and that buys 7 points of clarity and 7 of noise isolation.

The weak spot is the link. 88 on connection is below the $85 DJI Mic's 90, and per our data its 2.4GHz connection can struggle when the air is crowded with other wireless signals. If you film in packed venues, the Mic 3's 90 is the safer link.

## Best for presenters — DJI Mic 3 ($260)

**Top battery score, a steadier link**

The presenter slot weights clarity and connection stability most, then battery endurance. The Mic 3 scores 88, 90 and 95 on those, and 95 on battery is the best on this page. On these weights the Mic 2 actually scores a hair higher. It already holds the vlogger slot, so this one goes to the Mic 3.

It's $60 more than the Mic 2. That buys 2 points of connection and 2 of battery. The Mic 2 is ahead on clarity, 92 to 88, and on setup, 95 to 92, and the two tie on noise isolation and build. Per the listing it's the same layout: two transmitters, one receiver and a charging case.

So the drawback is plain. You pay $60 for a small lead in the two scores a long talk depends on. Per our data it fits best inside the DJI ecosystem. If you present now and then, the Mic 2 is enough.

## Best for filmmakers — Rode Wireless GO III (Black) ($160)

**32-bit float and 90 clarity for $160**

The filmmaker slot puts clarity first and connection stability second. The GO III scores 90 on clarity, second here only to the Mic 2's 92, and 88 on connection. Per our data it records 32-bit float, which leaves more room to fix levels in the edit.

On raw filmmaker scores the DJI Mic 2 and Mic 3 come first. Each kit appears once here, so the slot goes to the GO III. It's also the cheapest of the three: $40 less than the Mic 2 and $100 less than the Mic 3. It ties the Mic 2 on noise isolation at 85, and 92 on build and portability ties for the best here.

Battery is the drawback. 87 on battery endurance is the lowest on this page. Per our data its 2.4GHz band can also pick up interference in crowded wireless environments, the same caveat as the Mic 2.

## Also on the list — Hollyland Lark M2 ($70)

**The cheapest kit here, at $70**

At $70 the Lark M2 is the cheapest way onto this page, $15 under the DJI Mic. It ties for the best build and portability score here at 92, and battery endurance is 88. Its store rating is 4.7 stars, from more reviews than any other pick on this page.

The $15 step up to the DJI Mic buys a lot: clarity 85 against 78, connection 90 against 85, noise isolation 78 against 70. Its 78 on clarity and 85 on connection are both the lowest here.

Per our data the noise cancelling is aggressive and can make a voice sound less natural, so test it before a shoot that matters. If $15 is real money to you, this is a good kit. If it isn't, we'd spend it.

## FAQs

**Why does the budget pick cost more than the best overall pick?**

Because both are in the cheapest price band. The DJI Mic ($85) scored first overall, so the budget slot went to the best kit left in that band, the Rode Wireless Micro ($100). The two score almost the same. Per the listings, the Rode's extra $15 buys a second mic and a charging case; the $85 DJI Mic kit has one transmitter.

**DJI Mic, Mic 2 or Mic 3: which one should I buy?**

The $85 DJI Mic if you're recording yourself on a budget: it's a one-transmitter kit with the best noise isolation of our three picks at $100 or less. The $200 Mic 2 has the highest average score on this page and the best clarity, 92. The $260 Mic 3 adds 2 points of connection and 2 of battery over the Mic 2 and gives up 4 of clarity. If the budget stretches, the Mic 2 is where we'd stop.

**Which of these works best in a noisy place?**

The DJI Mic 2, the DJI Mic 3 and the Rode Wireless GO III, all at 85 on noise isolation, the best on this page. The $85 DJI Mic follows at 78. The Rode Wireless Micro, the Lark M2 and the $690 Shure sit at 70. Per our data the Shure's lavalier is omnidirectional, and the Lark's noise cancelling can make a voice sound less natural.

**Is the $690 Shure worth it over the DJI Mic 3?**

Only if the link is your problem. The Shure leads on connection stability, 92 to 90, and per our data it's dual-band. The $260 Mic 3 beats it on every other score. If you run presentations where a dropout isn't an option, that lead is what the extra $430 buys. For everything else, take the Mic 3.

**How many lavalier systems did you compare to get to these seven?**

164, every lavalier and wireless mic system in our database for this category. Each is scored 0 to 100 on six features: clarity and warmth, connection stability, setup simplicity, noise isolation, battery endurance, and build and portability. The overall ranking counts the six equally, with store ratings and price counted in; the three "best for" picks use their own weights.

## Methodology note
